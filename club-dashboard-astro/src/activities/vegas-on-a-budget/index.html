The gist

The whole game in 30 seconds. The rest of this page is detail.

  1. Budget your paycheck.$3,600 a month. Pick one option in each category. Nicer options earn stars, and you need at least 11: a budget has to be one you could live with.
  2. Life happens.The host spins the Wheel of Life. Tap the same event on your sheet; what it costs depends on what you picked.
  3. Bet on what you know.Eight money questions. Bet up to $100 a question on whether the answer is over or under the line.
  4. Most money wins.Savings + investments + chips − credit card debt. Your team sheet adds it up.

House rules

  • One phone per team.Pick one person to run the team sheet and type in your team name. Everyone else helps decide.
  • Build a budget you could live with.Nicer options earn stars, and your budget needs at least 11. Living on rice and the bus doesn't count.
  • Tap exactly what the wheel lands on.Every team gets the same life events. Some cost you more or less depending on what you picked in your budget.
  • Lock bets before the reveal.In Over/Under, pick over or under and lock your bet before the host shows the answer. Then tap what they revealed.
  • Stay on the same phone and browser.Your sheet saves on this phone only. Use a normal tab, not a private one, or it can vanish when the tab closes.

Money words

You'll hear these all game.

Take-home payWhat actually lands in your account after taxes. Your salary is bigger than your paycheck.
Needs vs. wantsA place to live is a need. The pool and the view are wants. On your sheet, the cheapest option in a category is the need.
50 / 30 / 20A starting point: about half of take-home on needs, under a third on wants, at least a fifth saved.
Emergency fundCash set aside only for surprises, like a repair or a doctor visit. It keeps a bad week off your credit card.
InvestingPutting money into things that can grow, like a fund that owns a slice of many companies. It can drop in the short run.
APRThe yearly interest rate on a loan or credit card. The higher it is, the more borrowing costs you.
Compound interestEarning interest on your interest. Slow at first, then fast. It works for you when you save and against you when you owe.
House edgeThe small slice a casino keeps from every bet, on average. It's built into every game.
