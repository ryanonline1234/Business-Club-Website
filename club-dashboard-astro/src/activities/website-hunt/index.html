Website Hunt

Three local businesses, three websites, 12 mistakes hidden on each. Find as many as you can before time runs out.

36to find

How to play

  • Play as a team.Open the round your presenter calls. They'll tell you how much time you have.
  • Look like a customer.Scroll the whole page, tap the buttons, and try it on a phone. Ask: would I trust this business with my money?
  • Write down every mistake.Use the “My list” button at the bottom of each website. Your list stays on your device.
  • Score points.When time's up, read your list out. 1 point for each mistake on the answer key. Most points after three rounds wins. Tie-breaker: the best one-sentence fix.
