About Us!!

Crumb & Co. was started in 1987 by my grandmother Rosa who always said "life is short, eat the bread." She came over with nothing but a sourdough starter and a dream and a very old cat named Biscuit (see "Meet Grandma's Cat" page). Over the years we have grown a lot and moved twice and had many many adventures which you can read about in our blog. We make many things like breads, cakes, cookies, pies, muffins and more, you name it we probaly make it!!

We take pride in our quality ingredients and our dedicated staff who work very hard every single day to make the best products for our valued customers in the community and beyond.

We are open most days, hours vary depending on the season and how busy we are, so check our Facebook page for updates or just stop by and see if we are open!! You can't miss us, we are right next to where the old video store used to be.
