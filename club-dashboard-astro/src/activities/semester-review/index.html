Semester Review

      Tap every team that got it:
      Final Bet

      Host it with no prep

      Put this page on the big screen and run it from the laptop. About 20 minutes for the board, plus 3 for the Final Bet.

      Read this out loud first

      “This is our whole semester on one board. Each column is something we did together: the AP Business class intro, the Jeopardy from our first meeting, Website Lab, and Vegas on a Budget. Every team answers every question: write A, B, C or D, and hold it up when I say. Right answers score the tile's points, and wrong answers cost nothing. At the end there's a Final Bet, where you can wager your points on one last question.”

      How to run it

      1. Split into teams of 3 or 4, each with paper to write a letter on. Tap Teams and add their names.
      2. A team picks a column and a value. Take turns picking.
      3. Read the question and the four choices out loud. Tap 20-second timer if you want a clock.
      4. Everyone holds up their letter at once. Tap Reveal the answer and read the explanation.
      5. Tap every team that got it, then Give points. If nobody did, tap Nobody got it.
      6. When the board is done or time is short, tap Final Bet. Teams secretly write a wager, you type them in, then show the last question.

      If something goes wrong

      • Wrong team or wrong tile? Undo takes back the last thing you did. Press it again to go further back.
      • A score is off? Teams has +100 and −100 for each team.
      • Closed the tab? The game saves in this browser. Reopen the same page on the same laptop.
      • Short on time? Skip straight to the Final Bet.

      Teams

      Teams of 3 or 4. Their names and scores show along the bottom of the board.
